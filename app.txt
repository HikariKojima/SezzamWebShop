Project: Sezzam construction ecommerce

Language:
- Bosnian only

Market:
- Bosnia and Herzegovina

Core idea:
- A clean, premium construction materials storefront where customers can browse products, add items to cart, and choose either online payment or cash/in-person payment.

Initial scale:
- Around 30 products at launch
- A few hundred users expected early on
- Admin-managed catalog and inventory

Primary customer flow:
1. Customer lands on home/catalog page.
2. Customer searches or filters products by category, unit, availability, and price.
3. Customer opens a product detail page.
4. Customer selects quantity based on product unit, such as m2, bag, piece, or pallet.
5. Customer adds product to cart.
6. Customer reviews cart.
7. Customer enters contact details.
8. Customer chooses payment method:
   - Cash/in-person payment
   - Online card payment
9. Customer submits order.
10. Customer receives confirmation.

Admin flow:
1. Admin logs in.
2. Admin creates and edits categories.
3. Admin creates and edits products.
4. Admin changes product prices.
5. Admin updates stock quantities.
6. Admin views orders.
7. Admin updates order status.

MVP pages:
- Home / materials catalog
- Product category page
- Product detail page
- Cart
- Checkout
- Order confirmation
- Admin login
- Admin dashboard
- Admin products
- Admin orders

MVP product fields:
- Name
- Slug
- Description
- Category
- Unit: m2, bag, piece, pallet, box, kg, ton
- Price
- Currency: BAM
- Stock quantity
- Low-stock threshold
- Images
- Active/inactive status
- Featured status

MVP order fields:
- Customer name
- Phone
- Email
- Address or pickup note
- Payment method
- Payment status
- Order status
- Order items
- Total price
- Created date

Payment approach:
- Support cash/in-person payment from the first functional checkout.
- Design the order model so online card payment can be added cleanly.
- Add online card payment after the basic checkout, inventory deduction, and order admin flow work.

Inventory approach:
- Track product stock quantity.
- Reduce stock after successful order submission.
- For online payment, reduce stock only after payment succeeds or reserve stock during payment.
- Show low-stock and out-of-stock states in the storefront.

SEO:
- Use clean product/category URLs.
- Add Bosnian metadata for home, categories, and product pages.
- Generate structured product data later if useful.

Design direction:
- Follow DESIGN.md and screen.png.
- Refined minimalist industrial storefront.
- Premium, clean, technical, calm.
- Avoid cluttered warehouse-style ecommerce UI.
